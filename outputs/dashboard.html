9. Interpretation limits

This dashboard does not produce a single final definition of life. It presents cross-observer invariants, local semantic branches, irreconcilable conflicts, untranslated residuals, task-indexed semantic contracts and proposed semantic dimensions. Moving beyond an individual observer reduces that observer's monopoly; it does not establish unconditional, context-free objective truth.
